# A5: Classification and unsupervised evidence

Choose one classification task.  Specify the action, false-positive and false-negative consequences, threshold, capacity, and at least one subgroup check.  Compare two classifiers with ROC, precision–recall, calibration, and a confusion matrix at the selected threshold.

For an unsupervised extension, cluster the feature representation and report the chosen preprocessing, number of clusters, silhouette score, stability check, and external evidence required before acting on any cluster.
